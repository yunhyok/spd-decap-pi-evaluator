## F3_unit_cell.png

`(i,j)`: four series in-plane edges `Z_edge = Z_s(f) + jωμ0·d_eff` (per square) to the N/S/E/W

adjacent conductor layer, a decap admittance branch `Y_decap(f) = 1/Z_SPICE(f)`, and a port
current-source branch. Element definitions from `RESEARCH_LOG.md` §3 ("채택 모델 정의"); purely
illustrative topology, not a rendering of a specific solved mesh.

## F5_dL_signature.png
`ΔL(f) = (Im Z_model − Im Z_ref)/ω` for 260729 Port18 over 30 kHz–10 MHz, for three model stages:
"S2 orig" (one-face internal-L formula, GND-only wall) and "EXP-4(b)" (two-sided symmetric
internal L, still GND-only wall) use the precomputed `dL_pH` arrays from
`results/exp4/result_S2_orig.json` and `results/exp4/result_S2_b.json` respectively; "EXP-8
cavity-wall" (final adopted model) is computed directly from `Z_im`/`Zref_im`/`freq` in
`results/exp8/result_260729_Port18_SITE0_any.json` (no `dL_pH` array is stored in that file).
Shows the near-constant excess-L plateau at low frequency for the GND-only variants and its
collapse to within ±3.3 pH once the cavity-wall rule is applied, matching the qualitative
description in `RESEARCH_LOG.md` §4 (EXP-4) and §5.

## F6_multirail.png
(a) 7-case bar chart of the 1 MHz complex error, GND-only(physical) vs cavity-wall(PowerSI
convention), read directly from `results/exp8/table.json` → `rows[*].gnd.err1M` /
`rows[*].any.err1M` (×100). (b) Scatter of predicted vs measured plane-L reduction (pH) per case:
x = `−predicted_dL_change_pH` from `results/exp8/prediction.json`; y = measured reduction
`gnd.dL1M − any.dL1M` from `results/exp8/table.json`, with the 1:1 line. The correlation label
"r = 0.965" is quoted verbatim from `results/exp8/EXP8_REPORT.md` ("실제 ΔL 변화와 예측의 상관은
0.965다"); recomputing Pearson r directly from the seven `(predicted, measured)` pairs plotted here
gives r = 0.946 (annotated on the figure alongside the report's number) — the small difference is
because the report's 0.965 could not be exactly reproduced from the two JSON fields alone (methodology
not fully specified in the JSON), so both numbers are shown rather than forcing agreement.
